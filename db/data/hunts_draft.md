# Two Vienna Treasure Hunts — Draft with Riddles (v3: cryptic hints too)

Two parallel 10-stop hunts for RubyConf Austria 2026. All stops outdoor, free, GPS-friendly. Same dark/mysterious voice, two distinct routes. Both `hunt_type: reward`.

**v3 hint policy:** no hint names the landmark. The player learns the actual name only after they GPS check in. Hints are three independent angles, revealed one at a time at random.

Each clue has:
- **Title** (1–3 words, fragment-like, never names the place)
- **Riddle** (1–2 sentences, one image, leaves the work to the player)
- **3 hints** — independent angles, no place name; `IncrementHintUsageService.unrevealed.sample` reveals them one at random per cooldown.

---

## Hunt 1 — "The Hidden City"

- **Description:** "Vienna keeps its secrets in plain sight. Ten stops trace the codes carved into the inner city — where Romans, Habsburgs, modernists and stonemasons hid their meaning in the buildings most people walk past."
- **Difficulty:** challenging
- **Hunt type:** reward
- **Completion message:** "You read what most visitors miss. Now claim the chest."

### Clue 1 · "The Drawn Curtains" — Looshaus (48.2076, 16.3661 · 25m)
> An emperor drew his curtains. Find what he could not bear to see.

Hints:
- An emperor closed his curtains so he wouldn't have to see it from the palace.
- Its architect wrote the essay 'Ornament and Crime' the same year.
- On a small square in the inner city, named for an archangel.

### Clue 2 · "Beneath the Cobbles" — Vindobona ruins (48.2076, 16.3664 · 25m)
> On a Habsburg square, the cobbles open into a glass pit. Two thousand years of soldiers are stacked between you and them.

Hints:
- A glass-walled circle set into the pavement of an old square.
- Soldiers slept here when Vienna had a Latin name.
- Excavated during U-Bahn construction. Look for the cobbles that open downward.

### Clue 3 · "Noon" — Ankeruhr (48.2117, 16.3744 · 25m)
> Wait under the bridge between two halves of one building. The hour strikes once a day, in procession.

Hints:
- Above an arcade between two halves of one building, a clock counts twelve.
- Twelve figures parade across once a day, at noon.
- Marcus Aurelius is said to have died on this square.

### Clue 4 · "The Lean" — Maria am Gestade (48.2128, 16.3704 · 30m)
> A church that bends. The river it leans toward is gone.

Hints:
- A Gothic spire on a steep medieval alley near the canal.
- Its name means 'on the bank' — but the bank is no longer there.
- Napoleon's troops used it as an arsenal.

### Clue 5 · "The Last Stone" — Mölker Bastei / Pasqualatihaus (48.2138, 16.3640 · 35m)
> The last stone of the broken wall. A deaf man wrote a symphony on top — and crossed out the name he had dedicated it to.

Hints:
- A scrap of the wall that broke the 1683 siege still stands above the modern street.
- Climb the stone staircase next to the University.
- Beethoven lived in the house on top while writing the *Eroica*.

### Clue 6 · "Bolts in Marble" — Postsparkasse (48.2102, 16.3795 · 40m)
> A bank that refused to hide its bones. Thousands of aluminum bolts hold up a marble face.

Hints:
- An architect refused to hide the bolts holding his marble façade together.
- 1906. Heated marble lobby visible through the glass entrance.
- On Georg-Coch-Platz, at the eastern edge of the Ring.

### Clue 7 · "Square and Compass" — Mozart-Denkmal (48.2057, 16.3651 · 30m)
> Behind an imperial garden gate, a man stands in marble. The flowers at his feet spell music; the carvings on his plinth spell a secret society.

Hints:
- A composer in marble, in the Hofburg's private garden.
- The flowerbed at his feet is a treble clef.
- Square, compass, and sun — the symbols of his most famous opera.

### Clue 8 · "Bloodline" — Spanische Hofreitschule (48.2074, 16.3666 · 35m)
> Behind a Baroque gate, white horses still trace the bloodline of an emperor's stable. Listen for hooves on sand.

Hints:
- Vienna's oldest equestrian institution, founded in 1572.
- White horses, bred in Piber, Styria.
- Behind a Baroque gate on Josefsplatz, next to the National Library.

### Clue 9 · "The Hero Who Left" — Theseustempel (48.2072, 16.3608 · 30m)
> A Greek temple in an imperial garden. Built to hold one statue. The statue moved. The temple stayed.

Hints:
- A Greek temple built to hold a single Canova sculpture; the sculpture moved to a museum.
- One-fifth scale of the Hephaisteion in Athens.
- In the rose garden between the Hofburg and the Burgtheater.

### Clue 10 · "Iron in Wood" — Stock im Eisen (48.2079, 16.3714 · 20m)
> Where two famous streets meet at the city's busiest corner, a piece of a tree stands behind glass. Five hundred years of strangers each left an iron mark.

Hints:
- A piece of a tree from 1440, behind glass at a building's corner.
- Travelling blacksmith apprentices each drove an iron nail into it for luck.
- On the corner where Graben meets Kärntnerstraße — Vienna's busiest crossroads.

## Hunt 2 — "Vienna Reinvented"

- **Description:** "Vienna is not all empire and waltz. Ten stops trace the buildings, blocks and monuments that refused the imperial line — the painters, planners and architects who reshaped the city out of spite, optimism, or pure aesthetic war."
- **Difficulty:** challenging
- **Hunt type:** reward
- **Completion message:** "You followed the city's quieter rebellion. Claim your reward."

### Clue 1 · "The Devil's Lines" — Hundertwasserhaus (48.2076, 16.3938 · 35m)
> A painter believed straight lines were the devil's. He was given a public housing block and proved it.

Hints:
- An architect renamed himself after rain — 'hundred-water.'
- A tree lives on its third storey.
- Onion domes on a council block, in Landstraße.

### Clue 2 · "Round the Corner" — KunstHaus Wien (48.2110, 16.3920 · 30m)
> Round the corner from the painter's most famous building, the same fevered hand reshaped an old factory.

Hints:
- Three blocks from the painter's most famous building, his second one.
- A former bentwood-chair factory.
- In the 3rd district, between the Hundertwasserhaus and the Donaukanal.

### Clue 3 · "Their Age, Their Art" — Secession (48.2007, 16.3656 · 30m)
> Above the door: *To every age its art.* Beneath the dome: two thousand five hundred leaves of gold.

Hints:
- Two thousand five hundred gilded laurel leaves on a windowless white dome.
- Built when young artists walked out of the Academy in 1898.
- South of the State Opera, at the edge of the Naschmarkt.

### Clue 4 · "Two Gates" — Otto Wagner Stadtbahn-Pavillons (48.2002, 16.3699 · 30m)
> Two matching gates, green and gold. The railway they served stopped running long ago. They were not built for emperors.

Hints:
- Two green-and-gold pavilions facing each other across a great square.
- Built for a metropolitan railway that closed in 1925.
- South of the State Opera, on either side of a fountain.

### Clue 5 · "The Compromise" — MuseumsQuartier (48.2030, 16.3589 · 50m)
> Two modern boxes inside an emperor's stables. Vienna fought hard enough that the boxes had to sink.

Hints:
- Modern cubes inside Fischer von Erlach's old imperial stables.
- Vienna fought so hard against the modernism that the cubes had to be sunk into the ground.
- Behind the twin Maria-Theresien-Platz museums.

### Clue 6 · "Four Giants" — Karl-Marx-Hof (48.2519, 16.3585 · 60m)
> A kilometer of utopia, painted yellow and red. Four giants stand watch above the central gate: Liberation, Care, Enlightenment, Strength.

Hints:
- A kilometer of social housing, painted yellow and red.
- Four giants over the central gate: Liberation, Care, Enlightenment, Strength.
- In Heiligenstadt, the 19th district, on the U4.

### Clue 7 · "The Hollow Drums" — Gasometer (48.1858, 16.4205 · 50m)
> Four brick cylinders that once held the city's gas. They sat empty for fifteen years. Then the basslines came.

Hints:
- Four brick cylinders, built in 1896 to store coal gas for the city's lamps.
- Empty for fifteen years; converted to apartments and offices in 2001.
- In Simmering, the 11th district, on the U3.

### Clue 8 · "The Burning Onion" — Müllverbrennungsanlage Spittelau (48.2363, 16.3573 · 40m)
> A chimney with a golden onion at the top. The painter who hated straight lines designed it. It burns the city's trash.

Hints:
- A golden onion crowns a working chimney.
- The painter who hated straight lines was asked to redesign it after a fire.
- On the Donaukanal, on the U4/U6 line just north of the Ring.

### Clue 9 · "The Forgotten Painter" — Strudlhofstiege (48.2229, 16.3596 · 30m)
> A staircase built for a painter no one remembers. A novelist took its name and made it famous — but not for the painter.

Hints:
- A Jugendstil staircase, built in 1910 for a forgotten Baroque painter.
- Heimito von Doderer made it the spine of a 900-page novel in 1951.
- In Alsergrund, between Liechtensteinstraße and the street it's named after.

### Clue 10 · "Twenty-Four Carats" — Johann-Strauss-Denkmal (48.2052, 16.3793 · 30m)
> Vienna's most-photographed man stands in a park, playing his violin for nobody. He is cast in twenty-four-carat gold.

Hints:
- Vienna's most-photographed monument, cast in twenty-four-carat gold.
- Edmund Hellmer, 1921. He plays his violin for nobody.
- In the Stadtpark, on the eastern Ring.
