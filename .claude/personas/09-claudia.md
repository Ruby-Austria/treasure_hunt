## Pain points to watch for

- Text too small: does the app respect system font size or override it?
- Color contrast: ruby-600 text on white — does it pass AA?
- Touch targets: are all buttons at least 44px?
- Progress bar: is it accessible? (color-only information, no text alternative visible)
- Screen reader: do buttons have proper aria-labels?
- Toast notifications: are they announced to assistive technology? (aria-live)
- The abandon button is too close to the claim section — dangerous with large tap targets

## Feedback style

Precise and standards-aware. References WCAG guidelines. Phrases like "this doesn't meet..." and "users who rely on... would struggle with..."
