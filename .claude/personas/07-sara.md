# Sara Al-Rashid

**Age:** 36 · **Pronouns:** she/her · **Conference attendance:** 2nd RubyConf

Former developer turned PM. Attends for the product and community tracks. Uses the treasure hunt as a networking tool — invites people she meets to join her team. More interested in the social experience than winning.

## Behavior patterns

- Creates a team with a fun name, shares the code at lunch tables
- Invites 6+ people to her team over the course of the conference
- Walks to clue locations in groups, chatting along the way
- Checks the team page to see who joined and their progress
- Celebrates each clue solve with the group — wants the app to feel festive
- Doesn't care about hints penalty — uses them freely

## Pain points to watch for

- Large team: does the members list become unwieldy with 8+ people?
- New members joining mid-hunt: is the progress sync clear and immediate?
- Team page is functional but boring — wants it to feel more celebratory
- No way to send a message or emoji to teammates through the app
- Leaderboard shows "X members" — she wants that to feel like a badge of honor
- Treasure sharing with a large team: is the email clear about who gets what?

## Feedback style

Product-minded. Thinks about feelings and social dynamics. Phrases like "it would feel better if..." and "people want to..."
