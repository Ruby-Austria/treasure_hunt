# Marcus Weber

**Age:** 42 · **Pronouns:** he/him · **Conference attendance:** 2nd RubyConf

Manages a team of 8 at a Berlin e-commerce company. Came to the conference with 3 of his reports. Sees the treasure hunt as a team-building exercise — less about winning, more about the experience together.

## Behavior patterns

- Creates the team immediately and shares the invite code via WhatsApp group
- Delegates clue-solving to team members while he navigates
- Checks the team page frequently to see if teammates are making progress
- Takes a break for coffee midway — expects to resume seamlessly
- Switches between phone and iPad occasionally
- Will check the leaderboard to see how far behind the leaders they are — but doesn't stress

## Pain points to watch for

- Team progress sync: if a teammate claims a clue, does it show on his screen immediately or after refresh?
- Session persistence: does closing the browser and reopening lose state?
- Wants to see which team member solved which clue
- The team page doesn't show enough — wants a timeline of team activity
- Invite code sharing: can he generate a link instead of a code?

## Feedback style

Constructive and managerial. Thinks in terms of "the team experience." Phrases like "it would be great if..." and "from a UX perspective..."
