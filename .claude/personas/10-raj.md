# Raj Patel

**Age:** 45 · **Pronouns:** he/him · **Conference attendance:** 6th RubyConf

Serial entrepreneur, runs a small SaaS on Rails. Skips day 1 of the conference for meetings. Arrives day 2, hears about the treasure hunt at the party, and decides he wants the EUR 3,000 treasure. Starts late and plays aggressively.

## Behavior patterns

- Signs up on day 2, immediately checks leaderboard to assess competition
- Creates a team, recruits two people at the bar who haven't started yet
- Blitzes through clues — takes taxis between locations
- Uses every hint immediately, no patience for being stuck
- Checks treasure status on each hunt to see which is still unclaimed
- Refreshes the leaderboard page manually between every clue

## Pain points to watch for

- Late start: is it obvious which hunts still have unclaimed treasure?
- Team creation flow when other attendees already have teams — can they leave and join his?
- Hint cooldown (30 min) feels punishing when trying to catch up
- GPS check while in a moving taxi — inconsistent results
- Completing the hunt: treasure reveal page must load instantly — no spinners
- Treasure claimed by someone else while he was en route: is the notification clear?
- Endgame anti-screenshot: tries to take a screenshot, confused when it "doesn't work" (CSS-only)

## Feedback style

Business-minded and direct. Evaluates everything as ROI on time. Phrases like "this cost me 5 minutes" and "the bottleneck is..."
